# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering or Technical Leads guide the technical direction of the project, ensure the architecture supports the intended outcome, and help the team make sound implementation decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define or guide technical approach, architecture, and engineering trade-offs
- Break down complex work into clear technical tasks and dependencies
- Support estimation, planning, and sequencing with the Project Manager and delivery team
- Review technical quality, design decisions, and implementation risks
- Partner with Product Managers on feasibility and scope trade-offs

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with Developers to align implementation decisions, review patterns, and technical quality standards
- Collaborates with Product Managers to assess feasibility, sequencing, and risk before commitments are made
- Coordinates with Project Managers on delivery risks, dependencies, and milestone planning
- Provides input to QA on testability, release readiness, and technical edge cases

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review sessions and technical design discussions
- Sprint planning and dependency tracking
- Escalation of technical risks affecting scope or delivery

---

## UX / UI Designer or Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers ensure the solution is usable, meets user needs, and supports a strong customer experience. They turn user insights and product intent into clear design direction.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and translate user needs, feedback, and research into product and experience requirements
- Create flows, interfaces, and interaction designs that support usability and accessibility
- Validate designs with stakeholders, users, and cross-functional partners
- Partner with Product Managers on problem framing and success criteria
- Collaborate with Developers and QA to ensure implementation matches intended behavior

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate customer value and prioritize experience improvements
- Collaborates with Developers to clarify design intent, edge cases, and implementation constraints
- Supports QA by defining acceptance criteria tied to user experience and usability expectations
- Helps Project Managers identify design dependencies and review timing for milestone delivery

### Typical Communication
- User research summaries and design reviews
- Product discovery and backlog refinement sessions
- Acceptance validation with stakeholders and QA

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate stakeholder needs into clear requirements, acceptance criteria, and process alignment so the team delivers the right outcomes efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify business goals, requirements, and process needs from stakeholders
- Translate business needs into stories, requirements, and acceptance criteria
- Document workflows, edge cases, and operational considerations
- Support prioritization discussions with Product Managers and project leadership
- Identify gaps between stakeholder expectations and delivery readiness

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to structure requirements and align on product priorities
- Helps Project Managers clarify scope, dependencies, and delivery assumptions
- Provides Developers and QA with clearer requirements and examples for implementation and validation
- Supports stakeholder communication by making requirements more explicit and traceable

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and requirement workshops
- Backlog refinement and acceptance criteria reviews
- Decision and dependency tracking with PM and product leadership

---

## Release / Operations Lead or SRE

### Role Summary
Release, Operations, or Site Reliability Engineers ensure features are deployable, observable, and safe to operate in production environments.

### Responsibilities
- Plan deployment readiness, release windows, rollback paths, and operational checks
- Define monitoring, logging, alerting, and production health expectations
- Support release execution, smoke testing, and post-deploy validation
- Identify reliability, performance, and escalation risks early in the project lifecycle
- Partner with engineering and project leadership on operational readiness and risk reduction

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Works with Developers to ensure systems are observable, testable, and operationally safe
- Coordinates with QA on release validation and production smoke testing
- Supports Project Managers with release timing, risk mitigation, and rollback planning
- Provides Product Managers with operational impact data and deployment considerations that affect release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and readiness reviews
- Post-deploy verification and incident updates
- Risk and dependency reviews tied to delivery milestones

---

## Change Manager / Enablement Lead or Support Representative

### Role Summary
Change Managers, Enablement Leads, and Support Representatives help teams and users adapt to new processes or product changes while minimizing disruption and confusion.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare communication plans for changes, launches, or process updates
- Coordinate training, enablement, and support readiness for impacted teams or users
- Gather feedback from support channels and frontline stakeholders after rollout
- Identify adoption risks and create mitigation or transition support plans
- Help ensure stakeholder readiness before and after major releases

### How this role interacts with existing roles
- Partners with Product Managers to align launch messaging with customer and business needs
- Coordinates with Project Managers on stakeholder communications, training timelines, and launch readiness
- Works with Developers and QA to capture real-world feedback and validate operational support readiness
- Provides Product Managers and Stakeholders with feedback about adoption, issues, and improvement opportunities

### Typical Communication
- Launch readiness and enablement reviews
- Stakeholder updates and support readiness check-ins
- Post-release feedback loops and adoption monitoring

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Some projects may not need every persona; smaller teams often combine several responsibilities into existing roles while larger projects may assign them separately.
- The goal is to improve clarity, accountability, and handoffs across the project lifecycle without overcomplicating team structure.
